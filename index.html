PSYCHOLOGY EXPERIMENT

Memory and Encoding Strategies

A short study of how different ways of processing words affect later recall.

Welcome

You will study 18 words in three blocks. Each block uses a different encoding strategy. After a short counting task, you will be asked to recall as many studied words as you can.

  • Study each word while it appears on screen.
  • Follow the strategy instructions shown before each block.
  • During the counting task, subtract 3 repeatedly from the starting number.
  • At the end, type all the words you remember, separated by spaces, commas, or line breaks.

This demo runs entirely in your browser. Responses are not uploaded or saved to a server. Download the CSV files at the end if you want to keep the session data.
