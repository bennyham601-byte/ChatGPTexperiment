# Memory and Encoding Strategies Experiment

## Run it
1. Keep `index.html`, `styles.css`, and `script.js` in the same folder.
2. Open `index.html` in a modern browser. No server, installation, or build step is needed.
3. Complete the experiment, then use the two download buttons on the results screen.

## Experiment details
- Three encoding strategies: maintenance rehearsal, self-reference, and mental imagery.
- Three six-word lists; strategy order and list-to-strategy assignment are independently shuffled for each session.
- Words are displayed one at a time for four seconds each.
- A 30-second backward-by-three distractor task follows the study blocks.
- Free recall is scored separately by encoding strategy.
- Matching is case-insensitive, accepts plural forms, and tolerates a one-character typo for words of at least five letters. It does not use semantic AI matching, so an unusual synonym may not receive credit.
- Summary CSV: one row per session.
- Trial CSV: 18 rows per session, one per studied word.

## Data and privacy
This is a static, client-side demonstration. It does not transmit data or save it to a server. CSV files are downloaded to the participant's device. Do not collect identifying or sensitive information unless your approved study protocol specifically requires it.

## Research-use note
This is a prototype, not a validated or ethics-approved research instrument. Before collecting research data, obtain any required instructor, institutional review board, or ethics approval; pilot the task; and document timing, scoring rules, and participant consent. The word lists are fixed across sessions, although their assignment to strategies is randomized.
